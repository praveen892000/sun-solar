### FAQ page (thorough) — `/faqs-pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli-yojana-rooftop-solar-scheme-for-residential-consumers-in-tamil-nadu/`

**Scheme:** PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana — free electricity goal via rooftop solar; up to ~300 units/month after subsidy; launched 13 Feb 2024; MNRE; outlay ₹75,021 crore; TN via TANGEDCO; national goal 1 crore homes by 2027.

**Eligibility:** Residential TANGEDCO connection; property owner; apartments OK with RWA approval; not commercial/industrial/agri; no income ceiling stated.

**Central subsidy amounts:**

| System | Subsidy |
| --- | --- |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW & above | ₹78,000 (capped) |

**Cost example (FAQ):** 3 kW ≈ ₹1.5–1.8 lakh before subsidy; net ≈ ₹72,000–₹1,02,000 after ₹78,000.

**Process:** pmsuryaghar.gov.in → TANGEDCO feasibility → empaneled vendor → net metering → subsidy to bank in ~30 days.

**Technical:** ALMM modules; panels ~25 years; inverters ~8–12 years; install 2–5 days after feasibility.

**Helpline:** 15555  

Closing CTA: Sri Sun Solar empaneled partner since 2013.
